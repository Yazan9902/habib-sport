Founder

Who Is Mansour Habib Allah?

Founder & CEO · Habib Sport Talents

From the streets of Ein Mahil in the Lower Galilee to the football pitch, Mansour began his journey in 1999 at the age of 13. His talent was discovered early, and he drew attention while playing alongside older teams, overcoming challenges with relentless determination.

Beyond the pitch, he chose to complete his path academically. He studied Football Business Management in Spain, then specialized in talent identification and scouting through PFSA in London. His learning journey continued in Barcelona, where he gained deeper training in Spanish football culture.

SBI University, Spain — Football Business Management

PFSA, London — Talent Identification and Scouting

Barcelona, Spain — Football Culture, Coaching, and Sports Nutrition

Connections with Real Madrid, Barcelona, Leganes, and Levante

19+ years of experience in physiotherapy · 8+ years working with children with ASD, ADHD, and overweight challenges

Our Vision

Vision and Goals

At Habib Sport, we aim to build a professional Arab sports generation that carries belief in its heart, a plan in its mind, and strength in its body. We believe football is a path to change and a gateway to real success stories.

Our Vision Includes

Expanding academy activity and opening branches in different Arab cities

Building strategic partnerships with international clubs and sports institutions

Preparing trained, educated talents for the global professional stage

Continuously developing programs according to the latest advances in sports science
